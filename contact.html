Questions before you contact us

Can I enquire before every detail is decided?

Yes. Start with what you know: the occasion, a preferred date, the location and an estimated guest count. Mention which details are still flexible.

What should I share when asking for a quote?

Include your date, venue, guest count, menu ideas and the support you need. Share a budget range if you have one, and ask for a breakdown of what the quote includes.

How do I check date availability?

Send your preferred date and location by WhatsApp or call us. If your date is flexible, include alternative dates so availability can be discussed.

What if guests have allergies or dietary preferences?

Mention these in your first enquiry, including the number of guests affected. Ask which requirements can be accommodated before agreeing on a menu.

What should I clarify before confirming?

Ask about the agreed menu or scope, timings, total cost, inclusions, payment schedule and cancellation terms. Confirm the details with the team before making a booking.
